# Code of conduct

This is a small project and the aim is a friendly place to ask, report and build.

- Be kind and assume good faith. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks, in any language.
- Keep issues and discussions on the project. Do not post other people's private information.
- Credit other people's work.

The maintainer can edit or remove comments, close threads and block people who do not follow this.

To report a problem, write privately to the maintainer, [aansonsa](https://github.com/aansonsa). If you have no other way to reach them, GitHub's private vulnerability reporting (the **Security** tab) also arrives there; mention that it is a conduct report.

This text is short on purpose. It follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
